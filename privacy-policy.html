Privacy Policy

TrackMyPlay is developed by Fengming Zhong. This policy describes how the app handles information when you use it.

What TrackMyPlay collects

TrackMyPlay does not require an account and does not collect personal information for a remote service.

Audio processing

Audio recognition happens on device to help detect matched instrument practice. Raw recordings are not uploaded, sold, or shared.

Local storage

The app stores practice metadata locally on your device, such as session duration, selected instrument context, settings, and calibration-related preferences. Removing the app removes that local data from your device.

Data sharing

TrackMyPlay does not share personal data with third parties because it does not maintain a remote personal-data account system for the app experience described on this site.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time, remove microphone permission through iOS settings, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to fretio0719@gmail.com.
